# Week 1: Wanjiku's Stall Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyses 14 days of sales and stock data for Wanjiku's market stall. The aim is to understand sales performance, monitor stock levels, calculate product profitability, and provide practical business recommendations.

## Objectives

- Calculate total and average daily sales.
- Identify the highest and lowest sales days.
- Compare sales performance between Week 1 and Week 2.
- Calculate weekly sales growth as a percentage.
- Identify products that need restocking.
- Calculate gross margin percentages for each product.
- Generate a formatted Stall Performance Report.

## Tools and Technologies

- Python
- Google Colab
- GitHub

## Data Structures and Functions

The project uses:
- **Lists** to store daily sales and corresponding days.
- **Dictionaries** to store product quantities, reorder levels, costs, and selling prices.
- **Functions** to perform reusable calculations and analyse stock performance.

## Key Business Insights

- Total recorded sales over 14 days: KES 46,450.
- Average daily sales: KES 3,318.
- Week 1 sales: KES 22,050.
- Week 2 sales: KES 24,400.
- Weekly sales growth: approximately 10.66%.
- Tomatoes and Sukuma are below their reorder levels.

## Business Recommendations

The analysis recommends monitoring stock levels, restocking products that are below their reorder levels, reviewing product profitability, and investigating the improvement in Week 2 sales.
